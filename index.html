Why One Piece is Worth Watching

The Overall Show

One Piece is a show that constantly gets barreated online for being long and not having good animation or story. It is true that it is preatty long, but in my opinion it is worth every second especially if you skip the filler. There are over 1000 episodes of One Piece with only a little over 100 episodes of filler. even if you skip the filler you are in for a long watch, however have you ever taken the time to think about how much time you spend watching shows? Take your favorite show for example, unless if it is new, unpopular, or cancelled it probably has over 60 hours at least. I know that it is still very long but that is roughly 200 episodes of One Piece but esspecially with the remake that they are making with will only be 500 episodes. That fixes the pacing, amount of episodes, and animation all in one. This is just one of many reason why I think One Piece is worth Watching.

The Story

People try to say that the story is the same thing. The main character goes to an island where the people are oppressed, stays and gets to know them, and eventually save and free them. This is true to some extent that is where the main character saves and frees the people. The difference comes with the fact that since they never watched the show they see the very end of an arc and go oh he freed the people again. However if you where to actually watch the show and know what liberation means you would know that there is a large list of places luffy helps but the people where never oppressed. People also think that there is no character dev again this is wrong. At the start of the show Luffy is strong and kind of aragent, but he quickly learns especially after Aces' death that he can't do anything and everything leading to him develpoing becoming a lot more serious when people he cares about can get hurt. This is just another of my reasons why I think One Piece is worth watching
